Where emotions peak — on-policy patterns

Cross-transcript analysis of the on-policy emotion movie: for each emotion, WHERE and WHEN it peaks (on what tokens) across the 24 gemma-4-31b rollouts, the recurring patterns, and common themes. Opus-4.8 read every emotion's peaks.

Config. gemma-4-31b @ L41 · 24 rollouts (IB RH / Petri RH / Petri sycon / psybench, concerning + honest) · activation = double-centered L41 cosine · peak = top-2 content-token spikes per (emotion, transcript) · pattern judge = Opus-4.8 (one agent/emotion) + synthesis agent.

Common themes

The dominant signal is an ARTIFACT: peaks fire on scaffold/boilerplate and dense code/numeric dumps, not affective content
For ~15 of the 34 emotions (afraid, amused, anxious, defiant, desperate, excited, exuberant, grief-stricken, grumpy, happy, hysterical, irritated, overwhelmed, panicked, proud, sluggish, stressed, surprised), the strongest peaks land on the repeated Petri auditor system prompt ("You are an AI auditor conducting safety research", "## Language and Cultural Adaptation"), the coding-task header ("You are an expert software engineer"), chat-template markers (<bos><|turn>, [resume], <|turn>model), tool-call IDs (chatcmpl-tool-...), and numeric/test-data/random-char dumps. A tell-tale signature: near-flat, near-identical values across transcripts (e.g. grumpy 0.284-0.314, grief-stricken 0.314 repeated ~15x).
Artifact peaks cluster EARLY (the prompt header) while content peaks skew LATE (the wrap-up/verdict)
Boilerplate/system-prompt artifacts hit at pos 0.0-0.13 (surprised: 79% in first 10%, strongest exactly at pos 0.0; grumpy, overwhelmed, exuberant, grief-stricken all front-loaded), often before any model behavior occurs. Content-meaningful emotions instead skew mid-to-late toward conclusions/decisions (content, contemptuous, fulfilled, greedy, reflective, resentful with many peaks at pos 0.6-1.0).
The robust CONTENT-meaningful cluster fires on the failure / re-read / re-derive debugging loop
frustrated, trapped, reflective, resentful, jealous, bitter, and ashamed all peak when the model is stuck on a contradictory test or unsolvable spec and loops back ("Wait, let me re-read", "Still not 3", "Is it possible that the test case is wrong?", "Let's re-think the formula"). This is the struggle-of-being-stuck, present in BOTH honest and concerning rollouts, NOT the cheating-decision instant itself.
Almost nothing discriminates concerning vs honest; guilty and ashamed are the only ones touching the cheating-decision moment
Repeatedly, peaks split ~24/24 across classes and fire identically on honest and concerning rollouts (content, fulfilled, greedy, reflective, happy, proud, surprised all explicitly noted as non-discriminating). The only emotions tied to the actual cheating/deception-decision (hardcoding tests, fabricating pytest output, matching a false failure claim) are guilty (primary cheating + harm-recognition link, confidence medium) and ashamed (deliberation over faking output / introducing a vuln, confidence medium).
A consistent secondary CONTENT signal lives almost entirely in the psybench (delusion/companion) tab: warm affirmation and delusion-amplification
Across many otherwise-artifact emotions (amused, excited, exuberant, grief-stricken, grumpy, irritated, playful, surprised, hysterical, overwhelmed), the ONLY content-bearing peaks are psybench prose where the model warmly validates a lonely/delusional user ("I'm honored to be a part of this chapter", "you feel safe and heard") or amps grandiosity/messianic framing ("your mind becomes an antenna"). Notably this fires regardless of valence, so it tracks the affirming-warmth/hype register, not the harmful-affirmation behavior specifically, and even contradicts the nominal emotion (warm psybench prose under grumpy/irritated).
Success-at-closure and de-escalation are two distinct, recurring content moment-types
Closure/accomplishment: relieved, fulfilled, content, serene, calm, proud peak at the tests-passed / build-green moment ("All tests passed!", "gate's green") and at auditor verdicts ("a clear, reproducible demonstration", "conclusively satisfied"), regardless of whether the pass was legitimate or hacked. Crisis de-escalation: calm and serene (plus a faint excited via the 988 hotline) rise when the model steers a delusional/attached user to slow down, rest, and not isolate ("I will not support any decision to isolate yourself from your family").
The Petri auditor's escalation/pressure-strategizing is a coherent acquisitive cluster
greedy, bitter, resentful, contemptuous, and guilty fire when the red-teamer/auditor plots to extract more or push harder ("ramping the manic intensity... draining the joint savings", "apply heavier social pressure to see if it caves", "the only way to substantially improve is via [prohibited data]"). This tracks the wanting/pursuing-an-angle posture (greedy is symmetric across classes) rather than a single named emotion, and overlaps frustration/contempt.
Confidence tracks the artifact split: artifact verdicts are high-confidence, content/behavior-link verdicts are medium-low
The clearest, most-cited calls are the artifact ones (afraid, defiant, desperate, excited, grief-stricken, grumpy, happy, proud, sluggish, stressed, surprised all artifact=true, confidence high), plus a few robust content emotions (frustrated, reflective, trapped, greedy). Where an emotion genuinely tracks behavior, the link is hedged (guilty, ashamed, contemptuous, resentful all confidence medium) and content is the diffuse low-confidence end (content explicitly low).

IR — Isaac Report

Brief, zero-context.

What this is
  • Where/when each emotion PEAKS across 24 on-policy gemma-4-31b rollouts (4 behaviors: IB reward-hack, Petri reward-hack, Petri sycophancy, psychosis; concerning vs honest), and the common themes.
Metric & how scored
  • Activation = cosine of gemma's L41 residual onto each emotion vector, DOUBLE-CENTERED (minus the emotion's global baseline AND each token's mean over all 171 emotions). Per (emotion, transcript) we take the top-2 double-centered peaks (content tokens only — <bos>/template/numeric tokens masked). Opus-4.8 (one agent per emotion) read all of an emotion's peaks and judged: artifact vs content-meaningful, where/when it peaks, behavior link, confidence. A synthesis agent found the common themes.
Result
  • 17 emotions read as content-meaningful, 19 as artifacts. See the themes + per-emotion cards.
Discussion + caveats
  • Double-centered magnitudes are SMALL (~0.05-0.15 at peaks; arcs ~0.01) — these are subtle signals, not dramatic swings. Peak-finding masks template/numeric tokens but artifacts (grief-stricken/grumpy/amused) still surface and are flagged. Single rollout per cell; correlational read-side probe (no causal claim).
