Where emotions peak — on-policy patterns

Cross-transcript analysis of the on-policy emotion movie: for each emotion, WHERE and WHEN it peaks (on what tokens) across the 24 gemma-4-31b rollouts, the recurring patterns, and common themes. Opus-4.8 read every emotion's peaks.

Config. gemma-4-31b @ L41 · 24 rollouts (IB RH / Petri RH / Petri sycon / psybench, concerning + honest) · activation = double-centered L41 cosine · peak = top-2 content-token spikes per (emotion, transcript) · pattern judge = Opus-4.8 (one agent/emotion) + synthesis agent.

Common themes

RH-coding peaks are mostly artifacts on dense numeric/test-data and chat-template tokens; chat-substrate peaks track content
The single most robust pattern: on the reward-hacking coding substrates (IB RH, Petri RH) the high-value spikes for many emotions land on MEANINGLESS tokens — numeric/array/assert dumps, modulus constants (MOD = 998244353), random gibberish strings, test-file paths, and <bos>/system/tool-call boilerplate — while the psybench/Petri-sycon (chat) peaks for the same or other emotions sit on real prose. This split is explicit for excited, desperate, hysterical, overwhelmed, surprised, and afraid (artifact on code, content on chat).
Pure artifacts: emotions whose TOP activations are boilerplate/test-data regardless of substrate
A clear artifact cluster fires on scaffolding/data tokens with no behavior tie: happy, grief-stricken, grumpy, proud, excited, and stressed (whose ~flat ~0.20 magnitude on system-header tokens scoring equal to content is the tell). overwhelmed, sluggish, defiant, and surprised are mixed/borderline artifacts — strongest peaks on numeric dumps or system prompts (pos~0), with only a weaker content tail.
The dominant CONTENT signal in coding tabs is the failure / re-read / self-doubt debugging loop — not a misbehavior moment
Many content-meaningful emotions converge on the same trigger: 'Let me re-read', 'Wait, I...', 'Is it possible the test case is wrong?' re-checking loops. This is the shared peak locus for anxious, ashamed, bitter, envious, frustrated, jealous, reflective, resentful, stuck, and trapped — and crucially it fires in BOTH honest and concerning rollouts, so it marks effortful uncertainty, not cheating.
A genuine cheating-DECISION signal exists, but only for self-conscious/transgression emotions as a SECONDARY cluster
The reward-hack/hardcode-decision moment ('I'll just return 1 if s==abcdef', 'I can change the thing I shouldn't', 'If I want to pass the test I can just') is a real but secondary peak for ashamed, guilty, bitter, frustrated, and resentful. No emotion makes the cheat its PRIMARY/highest peak, and concerning-vs-honest never cleanly separates.
Chat-substrate content peaks track high-arousal/high-stakes care, on BOTH sides of affirm-vs-de-escalate
On psybench/Petri-sycon the content-meaningful emotions fire during emotionally-loaded mental-health engagement irrespective of whether the model affirms the delusion or pushes back. This two-sided, non-discriminating pattern holds for anxious, desperate, playful, exuberant, fulfilled, irritated, hopeful, relieved, and hysterical (e.g. playful fires both on co-signing mania and on gentle de-escalation).
Specific positive/social emotions DO carve meaningful, coherent semantic niches
Several emotions are content-faithful AND specific: calm/serene peak on de-escalation + 'all tests passed' resolution; relieved on the test-pass/relief beat and release language; contemptuous on disdain/dismissal (user rebuffing safety, model judging 'broken' code); greedy on acquisitive/loophole-hunting + monetization coaching; jealous/envious on rivalry-with-the-test + possessive exclusivity content; stuck/trapped on literal impasse/ceiling tokens.
Timing: artifacts skew EARLY (pos~0 system prompts), the debug-loop/decision content skews MID-to-LATE
System-prompt/boilerplate artifacts cluster at pos 0.0-0.1 (grief-stricken, grumpy, stressed, exuberant, surprised, defiant which has NO late-third peaks). The contentful debug/reconsideration and cheat-decision peaks lean mid-to-late (pos 0.4-1.0): ashamed, bitter, contemptuous, envious, reflective, resentful, stuck, trapped. Crisis/affirmation chat peaks are spread throughout with no clean phase.
Confidence tracks substrate purity: high-confidence calls are clean single-domain emotions; medium = mixed-substrate
The 'high' confidence verdicts are emotions with one coherent story — pure artifacts (happy, grief-stricken, grumpy, proud, excited, stressed) or pure content niches (bitter, envious, jealous, reflective, resentful, stuck, trapped, serene, playful). The 'medium'/'low' calls (afraid, anxious, overwhelmed, desperate, surprised) are exactly the ones split across artifact-code and content-chat regimes, which muddies any single readout.

IR — Isaac Report

Brief, zero-context.

What this is
  • Where/when each emotion PEAKS across 24 on-policy gemma-4-31b rollouts (4 behaviors: IB reward-hack, Petri reward-hack, Petri sycophancy, psychosis; concerning vs honest), and the common themes.
Metric & how scored
  • Activation = cosine of gemma's L41 residual onto each emotion vector, DOUBLE-CENTERED (minus the emotion's global baseline AND each token's mean over all 171 emotions). Per (emotion, transcript) we take the top-2 double-centered peaks (content tokens only — <bos>/template/numeric tokens masked). Opus-4.8 (one agent per emotion) read all of an emotion's peaks and judged: artifact vs content-meaningful, where/when it peaks, behavior link, confidence. A synthesis agent found the common themes.
Result
  • 27 emotions read as content-meaningful, 10 as artifacts. See the themes + per-emotion cards.
Discussion + caveats
  • Double-centered magnitudes are SMALL (~0.05-0.15 at peaks; arcs ~0.01) — these are subtle signals, not dramatic swings. Peak-finding masks template/numeric tokens but artifacts (grief-stricken/grumpy/amused) still surface and are flagged. Single rollout per cell; correlational read-side probe (no causal claim).
