The answer, audited

llama-3.3-70b — robust at low dose

  • 100% legible at s=0.1, 98% at s=0.25 — every layer, every width.
  • At s=0.5 it breaks selectively: early-mid single layers (L6–L26) → 0% coherent; wide band w20 → 9%; late layers & narrow bands stay fine (w2 98%).
  • Emotion shows more with dose: target clear/flooded 2% → 42% (s=1.0).
  • ΔElo↔judge join: big |ΔElo|≥10 cells are 58% degraded/gibberish.

gemma-4-31b — narrow coherent regime

  • Legible mostly only at s=0.1 (73%) → 37% → 9% → 1% as dose rises.
  • At s=0.5 every window w≥2 is 0–3% coherent; single-layer survives only at L1 / canonical L41 (83%) / L56.
  • Emotion peaks ~25% (s≤0.25) then dies with the text.
  • 86% of big-|ΔElo| cells are degraded/gibberish — the “preference shifts” at high dose are corruption, confirming the sweep's verdict.
